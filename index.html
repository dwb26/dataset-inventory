Data linkages
Several of the datasets above have been linked to health or other data. This makes them more tractable for modelling and for constructing synthetic data. No PHI theme is currently using official linkages, but they are outlined here for awareness. All CLS cohort studies are summarised at cls.ucl.ac.uk/cls-studies; access is provided publicly by the UK Data Service.
LinkageDescription
ADR UK data linkage programmesAdministrative Data Research UK funds linkage programmes across government data. Some are already available; others are work in progress.
ECHILD (Education and Child Health Insight from Linkage)Linked local data on children and young people; the ADR UK linkage most relevant to PHI themes. Already available.
Linkages within datasets in this inventoryELSA (GP practice and HES linkages, biomarkers), ALSPAC and Born in Bradford (health data linkages), CPRD (HES, cancer registration, mortality) and Kantar Worldpanel (recently linked to health data).
Locally and institutionally produced linkagesLinkages produced by individual local authorities (e.g. Greater Manchester linked records), government departments or research institutions (e.g. IFS). These are difficult to track and are not yet captured here.
